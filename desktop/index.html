First-run setup

Create your iPhone.

Studio keeps a private iOS device on your computer. Apple firmware is never included in the public installer.

Download & prepare from Apple

Downloads the pinned iPhone X firmware directly from Apple's servers and builds your local device. Allow at least 35 GB of free storage and 6 GB of available memory. No Docker, WSL or Apple developer keys are required.

Already have a prepared iOS root image?

Import a 16 GiB iOS Sweet HFSX root (.raw / .img). Studio uses a private copy and installs its app-management agent.
